# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in ContextPro's issues, pull requests and community spaces is expected to help make collaboration respectful and useful, regardless of background, identity or experience.

Discuss ideas and code without attacking people. Give specific, constructive feedback, welcome questions and respect different levels of experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information or continue unwanted contact. Sexual harassment, impersonation and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, warn participants or restrict access. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect those affected. Repeated or serious violations can result in a ban. Maintainers are accountable to the same standards.

For content that violates GitHub policy, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls. For non-sensitive moderation concerns, open a short issue asking for maintainer help; do not publish private evidence or personal details. If a private channel is needed, ask the maintainer to establish one before sharing details. A participant may request reconsideration with relevant context through the same reporting route; do not contact another participant who has asked you to stop.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public moderation reports. This policy is original project text and is distributed under the repository's MIT license.
